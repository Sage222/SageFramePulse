Windows 10 / 11 Usage Guide

01. Testing 3rd-Party FPS Limiters Set your external frame limiter (RTSS, NVIDIA Control Panel Max Frame Rate, or AMD Chill) to cap your browser or global 3D profile, select the matching FPS cap on the left, set intensity to Light, and press Start.
02. 60-Second Overclock Stability Pass Choose Balanced or Heavy intensity, label your clock profile (e.g. Stock vs +150 MHz Core), and click Run 60s Overclock Benchmark to log and compare 1% Lows and frame pacing jitter.
03. Anti-Freeze Watchdog Protection Per-frame ray-marching is bounded to a safe ceiling of 56 steps and automatically steps down if frame time exceeds 58 ms (<17 FPS), ensuring Windows and your mouse cursor never become unresponsive.
