Notes on Browser V-Sync (120Hz Cap), 300+ FPS Mode & 60s Overclock Testing

01. Why Browsers Normally Cap at 120 FPS (V-Sync) Standard HTML5 animations use requestAnimationFrame, which web browsers lock to your monitor refresh rate (120 FPS on a 120Hz monitor). FramePulse uses an Uncapped MessageChannel + WebGL Flush Loop by default so your GPU renders at 300+ FPS without waiting for vertical blank.
02. Hitting 300+ FPS vs 60s Overclock Stress Use Ultra-Light (1–2 shader steps at 25%–50% resolution) to eliminate fragment bottlenecks and hit 300–600+ FPS while testing your 3rd-party FPS limiter. Switch to Balanced or Heavy (OC) for 60-second GPU overclock stability runs.
03. Anti-Freeze Watchdog Protection Per-frame ray-marching is bounded to a safe ceiling of 56 steps, yields to browser input events every 18 ms, and automatically steps down if frame time exceeds 58 ms (<17 FPS).
